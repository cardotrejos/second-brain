Room prompt 4x4 Interior Design Style Grid Prompt (Room-Agnostic)

“Sixteen Styles, One Space”
“Professional architectural photography 4x4 grid showcasing identical room transformed through 16 distinct interior design styles. Camera: consistent 3/4 angle, 24mm tilt-shift lens, f/8, tripod-mounted. Each frame applies complete style transformation to the same space.
Row 1 – Classic & Traditional:
	1.	Scandinavian Minimalist — Light oak wide-plank flooring, white walls with warm undertone, low-profile oatmeal linen upholstery, black metal accents, sheepskin textiles, ceramic vessels, sheer white curtains, single statement pendant. Palette: whites, warm grays, natural wood, matte black. Lighting: bright, airy, natural light dominant. Mood: calm, functional, hygge warmth.
	2.	Mid-Century Modern — Walnut herringbone flooring, white walls with single mustard accent wall, tapered leg furniture in teak, iconic sculptural seating, sunburst mirrors, geometric patterns in burnt orange and cream, brass sputnik fixtures, monstera plants. Palette: teal, mustard, burnt orange, walnut, brass. Lighting: warm afternoon glow. Mood: retro optimism, sophisticated playfulness.
	3.	French Parisian — Chevron light oak parquet, soft gray walls with ornate white molding and panel details, marble surfaces, velvet tufted upholstery in dusty blue, gilded mirrors, crystal chandeliers, antique curved-leg furniture, fresh flowers, floor-length silk drapes. Palette: soft gray, dusty blue, gold, marble white, blush. Lighting: romantic diffused daylight. Mood: elegant, romantic, timeless sophistication.
	4.	Traditional English — Rich mahogany hardwood, Persian rugs in deep reds and navy, hunter green walls with white wainscoting, rolled-arm Chesterfield leather seating in cognac, brass library lamps, built-in bookshelves, oil paintings in gilt frames, tartan textiles, heavy damask curtains. Palette: hunter green, cognac, deep red, navy, brass. Lighting: warm pools of lamplight. Mood: scholarly, heritage, gentlemen’s club warmth.
Row 2 – Contemporary & Modern:
	1.	Contemporary Minimalist — Polished concrete flooring, pure white walls, low modular seating in charcoal, single oversized abstract monochrome art, hidden storage, black metal and glass surfaces, architectural floor lamp, motorized white blinds. Palette: white, charcoal, black, concrete gray. Lighting: clean, even, recessed. Mood: serene, uncluttered, gallery-like calm.
	2.	Industrial Loft — Sealed concrete floors, exposed red brick walls, raw steel elements, distressed tobacco leather seating, reclaimed wood on pipe legs, Edison bulb clusters, metal factory shelving, vintage objects, worn area rugs. Palette: brick red, raw steel, tobacco, aged wood, black iron. Lighting: warm tungsten Edison glow, intentional shadows. Mood: raw, urban, warehouse authenticity.
	3.	Modern Farmhouse — Whitewashed oak plank flooring, white shiplap walls, natural cotton slipcovered seating, reclaimed beam accents, woven baskets, galvanized metal details, linen curtains on iron rods, stoneware vessels, cozy knit throws. Palette: white, cream, warm gray, natural wood, black iron. Lighting: bright and fresh, wrought iron fixtures. Mood: cozy, collected, modern rusticity.
	4.	Urban Modern — Dark espresso hardwood, charcoal textured walls, sleek white Italian leather seating, chrome and glass surfaces, large-scale black and white photography, geometric black/white/gray rugs, layered sheer and blackout curtains. Palette: charcoal, white, chrome, black, cool gray. Lighting: layered recessed and LED strips, dramatic. Mood: sophisticated, city penthouse, nightlife elegance.
Row 3 – Global & Cultural:
	1.	Japanese Zen — Tatami-style natural flooring, off-white lime-washed walls, low platform seating in charcoal linen, shoji screen dividers, bonsai on simple stands, calligraphy scroll art, floor cushions, black iron teapot, rice paper window panels. Palette: off-white, charcoal, bamboo, black, moss green. Lighting: soft diffused, contemplative. Mood: meditative, balanced, wabi-sabi beauty.
	2.	Moroccan Bohemian — Terra-cotta geometric zellige tile, warm textured stucco walls, low carved wooden seating with jewel-tone cushions, brass lantern pendants, ornate wooden screens, embroidered leather poufs, bone inlay mirrors, layered vintage rugs. Palette: terra-cotta, sapphire, ruby, gold, turquoise. Lighting: lanterns casting patterned shadows. Mood: exotic, layered, sensory richness.
	3.	Mediterranean Coastal — Tumbled limestone flooring, white textured plaster walls with arched niches, deep natural white linen seating, driftwood surfaces, terracotta pots with olive branches, iron candelabras, billowing white gauze curtains, blue/white ceramics, seagrass rugs. Palette: white, terracotta, Mediterranean blue, olive green. Lighting: bright sun-drenched, golden. Mood: relaxed, sun-kissed, Aegean escape.
	4.	Indian Maximalist — Inlaid marble flooring, deep peacock blue walls, ornate carved wooden seating with silk cushions in marigold and fuchsia, brass figurines, embroidered floor bolsters, carved jali screens, hanging brass bells, marigold garlands, gold-embroidered silk curtains. Palette: peacock blue, marigold, fuchsia, gold. Lighting: rich warm brass lamp glow. Mood: opulent, celebratory, sensory abundance.
Row 4 – Specialty & Emerging:
	1.	Art Deco Revival — High-gloss black lacquered flooring, deep navy walls with geometric gold molding, curved emerald velvet seating, sunburst brass mirrors, stepped geometric shelving, marble and brass accents, frosted glass sculptural lamps, gold velvet drapes with black trim. Palette: emerald, navy, gold, black, marble. Lighting: glamorous wall sconces, dramatic. Mood: lavish, theatrical, 1920s grandeur.
	2.	Biophilic Modern — Light cork flooring, living moss wall installation, organic-shaped sage green bouclé seating, natural edge wood surfaces, hanging planters at multiple heights, terrariums, stone pebble accents, abundant air-purifying plants, undyed natural linen curtains. Palette: every green shade, natural wood, stone gray, cork. Lighting: greenhouse-like, supplemental grow lights. Mood: alive, oxygen-rich, nature immersion.
	3.	Japandi Fusion — Light ash flooring, warm white walls with single black accent stripe, low streamlined warm gray wool seating, pale wood with black metal frame surfaces, single large ceramic vessel, paper pendant lights, bonsai, ikebana arrangements, warm beige linen curtains. Palette: warm white, ash, black accent, pale wood, sage. Lighting: bright but soft, material-focused. Mood: refined simplicity, warm minimalism.
	4.	Avant-Garde Maximalist — Checkered black/white marble flooring, colorful Memphis-style geometric wall patterns, curved hot pink velvet seating, wavy asymmetrical mirrors, abstract sculptural lighting, clashing patterns throughout, salon-style art collection, neon sign art, mixed metals. Palette: hot pink, electric blue, yellow, black, white. Lighting: theatrical, neon accents, spotlighting. Mood: bold, rule-breaking, creative chaos.
Post-processing: individual color grading per style mood. Output: high-resolution architectural portfolio quality. Full spectrum from serene minimalism to opulent maximalism.”
