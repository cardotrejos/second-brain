Each item should have this:

- **Title**: The name of the item or artifact.
- **Description**: A detailed, text-based description of the item, potentially enhanced by AI-generated insights.
- **Categories**: Broad categories or classifications the item belongs to (e.g., textiles, ceramics, manuscripts).
- **Tags**: Keywords to help with search and filtering (e.g., "Victorian," "restored," "donated").
- **Provenance**: Information about the item’s history or origin, including previous ownership or discovery details.
- **Condition**: Current state of the item, along with any restoration history; may include a rating or descriptive text.
- **Date Acquired**: When the item was added to the collection.
- **Location**: The current storage or display location of the item within the facility.
- **Photographs**: Visual records of the item, supported by AI-enhanced descriptions.
- **Dimensions**: Measurements of the item where applicable.
- **Materials**: Information on the materials or medium used in the item's creation.
